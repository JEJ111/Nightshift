NightShift 0.5.7

October 30 compatibility release for Nivalis Nights Steam build 26295359, Unity 2020.3.49f1.

Install or update

  1. Save and close the game.
  2. Download the complete ZIP from the NightShift website.
  3. Right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, and open the extracted folder.
  4. Double-click Install NightShift.exe. Existing NightShift users do not need to uninstall first.
  5. Launch through Steam and load your save.

F11 cycles frame generation Off, 2x, 3x, 4x and Off; F5 toggles DLAA; F7 shows or hides the menu. DLAA starts on and frame generation starts off. DLSS upscaling is coming soon.

Player-verified DLAA and mode-switch gameplay; 45 installer/preservation and nine native checks passed. No controlled FPS or physical-pacing benchmark is claimed. See payload/INSTALL.md for requirements and payload/STATUS.md for validation limits.
